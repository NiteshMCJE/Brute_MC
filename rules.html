Server Rules

Follow these to keep Brute MC fun for everyone.

  • Be respectful — no harassment, hate speech, or toxicity.
  • No cheating — X-ray, kill aura, macros, or unfair mods = ban.
  • No exploiting gems, bugs, or dupes — report them, don't abuse them.
  • No griefing, stealing, or spawn-camping.
  • No advertising other servers or spamming chat.
  • Cracked clients are welcome, but alt-raiding and ban evasion are strictly prohibited.
  • Follow staff instructions — they have the final call.
  • Keep it respectful — no harassment, discrimination, or NSFW content.
  • No spam, self-promo, or unsolicited DMs.
  • Keep channels on-topic — use the right channels for the right topics.
  • No leaking personal info (Doxxing).
  • English in main channels for moderation clarity.
  • No ban evasion — appeals go through the ticket system.
  • Follow Discord Terms of Service.
